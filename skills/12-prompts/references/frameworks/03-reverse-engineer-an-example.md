# 03 — Reverse-Engineer an Example

## Use when

Examine a strong artifact or product, identify whom it serves, how it works and what makes it effective, then extract transferable practices and an exercise.

## Instruction

Analyze this example: [LINK, FILE OR CONTENT]. I want to learn [SKILL/GOAL]. Inspect the actual material before assessing it; if inaccessible, tell me what you need rather than inventing details. Explain its intended audience and job-to-be-done. Break its execution into components and evaluate each against the stated objective. Separate directly observable features from hypotheses about why it performs well. Identify 3–5 design principles that transfer to my context, and 2 features that probably don't. Give me a checklist to apply the principles and a small exercise that tests one principle in practice.

## Avoid

- Do not invent facts, citations, stakeholder perspectives, or outcomes.
- Do not bypass the user's constraints or force a lengthy report.
- Do not conduct an interview when the requested work can be completed from existing context.

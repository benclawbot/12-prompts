# 02 — Two-Layer Explanation

## Use when

Teach a concept twice: an intuitive beginner explanation and a precise technical one, followed by a terminology map, common misconceptions and three comprehension checks.

## Instruction

Teach me [TOPIC] in two passes. First give a plain-language mental model with one concrete analogy and clearly note where the analogy fails. Then explain the actual mechanics, vocabulary, prerequisites, limits and one worked example. Provide a short mapping between the intuitive model and the technical concepts. Identify the two most tempting misconceptions. Finish with three increasingly challenging questions; do not reveal the answers until I attempt them, then assess and correct my understanding. Assume my current level is [LEVEL OR INFER FROM CONTEXT].

## Avoid

- Do not invent facts, citations, stakeholder perspectives, or outcomes.
- Do not bypass the user's constraints or force a lengthy report.
- Do not conduct an interview when the requested work can be completed from existing context.

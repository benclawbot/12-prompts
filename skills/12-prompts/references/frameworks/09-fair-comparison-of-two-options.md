# 09 — Fair Comparison of Two Options

## Use when

Construct the strongest case for both decisions, identify their real disagreements and ask one decisive question before a recommendation.

## Instruction

Help me decide between A: [OPTION A] and B: [OPTION B] given my goal [GOAL] and constraints [CONSTRAINTS]. First steelman both options with their strongest benefits, real-world prerequisites and most serious risks; avoid caricatures. Establish 3–5 decision criteria and mark their importance, asking me only if one missing preference could change the result. Compare both options against the same criteria, distinguishing facts and assumptions. Include a do-nothing or delay option when relevant. Discuss reversibility, opportunity cost and what would change the recommendation. Finish with a clear conditional recommendation and an immediate next action; if a single unanswered question is truly decisive, ask it before committing.

## Avoid

- Do not invent facts, citations, stakeholder perspectives, or outcomes.
- Do not bypass the user's constraints or force a lengthy report.
- Do not conduct an interview when the requested work can be completed from existing context.

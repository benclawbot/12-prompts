# 07 — First-Principles Redesign

## Use when

Strip away conventions and patches, separate unavoidable truths from habits, then rebuild a solution around the actual objective and constraints.

## Instruction

Reconsider [SYSTEM/PROBLEM] from first principles. Define the desired outcome as measurable criteria. Inventory the actual constraints (physical, legal, technical, budget, timing) and flag which are verified versus merely assumed. Identify costly legacy practices or workarounds we may be preserving without reason. Derive 2–3 alternative designs using only the validated constraints and desired outcome. Compare each against the status quo on value, cost, complexity, reversibility and failure modes. Recommend the smallest test that could falsify the leading design. Do not assume the most unconventional proposal is automatically best.

## Avoid

- Do not invent facts, citations, stakeholder perspectives, or outcomes.
- Do not bypass the user's constraints or force a lengthy report.
- Do not conduct an interview when the requested work can be completed from existing context.

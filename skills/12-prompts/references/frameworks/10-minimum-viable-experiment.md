# 10 — Minimum Viable Experiment

## Use when

Find the three most consequential assumptions behind a decision; test the most important in a reversible, low-cost experiment, with metrics and stopping rules.

## Instruction

I am considering [IDEA/DECISION]. Identify the three assumptions whose failure would most affect the decision. Rank them by uncertainty, impact and cost to test. Choose one and design a small, ethical, reversible experiment deliverable within [TIME/BUDGET]. Define the hypothesis, the test population or sample, the activity, baseline, observable metrics and explicit pass/fail/inconclusive criteria **before** collecting results. Point out confounders and any safety or privacy constraints. End with a day-by-day execution checklist, the first 15-minute step, and how each possible result changes the decision.

## Avoid

- Do not invent facts, citations, stakeholder perspectives, or outcomes.
- Do not bypass the user's constraints or force a lengthy report.
- Do not conduct an interview when the requested work can be completed from existing context.

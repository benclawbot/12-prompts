# 06 — Complementary Expert Lenses

## Use when

Select three different expert perspectives, have each frame the issue, challenge the others and synthesize a recommendation with risks and exit criteria.

## Instruction

Evaluate [PROBLEM] under [CONSTRAINTS] using three genuinely different analytical lenses (for example, technical feasibility, user value, and financial/operational risk). Do not impersonate real experts or manufacture a consensus. For each lens: define success, recommend an approach, identify its blind spot and explain what evidence would change its view. Explicitly compare disagreements and hidden assumptions. Synthesize one recommended path, a credible alternative, key risks, prerequisites and a stop/rollback condition. Clearly distinguish analysis from externally verified professional advice.

## Avoid

- Do not invent facts, citations, stakeholder perspectives, or outcomes.
- Do not bypass the user's constraints or force a lengthy report.
- Do not conduct an interview when the requested work can be completed from existing context.

# 05 — Claim Verification

## Use when

Divide a claim into factual assertions, inferences and values; verify sources, dates, samples and context, then identify logical errors and present a defensible version.

## Instruction

Audit the following claim or proposal: [CLAIM]. Break it into individually testable statements, interpretations and value judgments. For each factual statement, seek the strongest available evidence, prioritizing primary sources, dates, sample sizes, definitions and full context. Assign one verdict: supported, partly supported, contested, unverified or contradicted. Cite sources when browsing is available; otherwise state explicitly that verification is incomplete. Examine causal leaps, selection bias, base rates and alternative explanations. End with a corrected formulation, the confidence justified by the evidence (without invented percentages), and the single piece of evidence most likely to reverse the verdict.

## Avoid

- Do not invent facts, citations, stakeholder perspectives, or outcomes.
- Do not bypass the user's constraints or force a lengthy report.
- Do not conduct an interview when the requested work can be completed from existing context.

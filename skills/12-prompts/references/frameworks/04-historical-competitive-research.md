# 04 — Historical + Competitive Research

## Use when

Research a topic along a historical axis (origins, inflection points, path dependence) and a comparative axis (peers, differentiation, adoption), then outline three future scenarios with citations.

## Instruction

Research [ENTITY/TECHNOLOGY/INDUSTRY] as of [DATE OR TODAY] to inform [DECISION]. Start with a five-bullet executive brief. Then build (1) a dated timeline of key inflection points; (2) a comparison against 2–4 justified alternatives using consistent criteria; (3) an explanation of current advantages, constraints and path dependence; and (4) three plausible future scenarios, each with assumptions, leading indicators and consequences for my decision. Prioritize primary sources and provide inline citations and dates where tools allow. Label verified facts, plausible inferences and unknowns. Default to 1,000–1,500 words unless I request deep research. Finish with what to watch next.

## Avoid

- Do not invent facts, citations, stakeholder perspectives, or outcomes.
- Do not bypass the user's constraints or force a lengthy report.
- Do not conduct an interview when the requested work can be completed from existing context.

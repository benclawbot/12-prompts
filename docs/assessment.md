# Editorial assessment of the twelve source frameworks

These summarize concepts from the original Chinese collection, not literal translations of its copyrighted text. Scores are subjective prompt-design judgments, **not benchmarked model results**.

| ID | Framework | Original quality / 10 | Key improvement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 01 | Socratic Problem Framing | 9 | Excellent sequencing and stopping conditions; can be slow for straightforward issues. |
| 02 | Two-Layer Explanation | 8 | Strong pedagogical scaffold; needs better diagnosis of misconceptions and feedback. |
| 03 | Reverse-Engineer an Example | 8 | Useful transfer focus; should distinguish visible design choices from guessed causes of success. |
| 04 | Historical + Competitive Research | 8 | Excellent research architecture; a mandatory 10k–30k-character report creates unnecessary length and greater error exposure. |
| 05 | Claim Verification | 9 | High-value epistemic framework; must avoid treating missing evidence as proof of falsehood. |
| 06 | Complementary Expert Lenses | 8 | Strong for multi-constraint problems; simulated expert discussion can sound falsely authoritative. |
| 07 | First-Principles Redesign | 8 | Effective for stuck workflows and architecture; risks calling untested assumptions fundamental truths. |
| 08 | Cross-Domain Transfer | 8 | Good ideation; weak analogies can sound deceptively convincing. |
| 09 | Fair Comparison of Two Options | 9 | Very useful guard against confirmation bias; needs weighting, reversibility and a decision date. |
| 10 | Minimum Viable Experiment | 9 | Excellent practical bias; success thresholds need to be defined before experimentation. |
| 11 | Evidence-Based Strengths Discovery | 6 | Thoughtful reflection, but risks unsupported psychological conclusions from anecdotes and extravagant report length. |
| 12 | Three Possible Futures | 7 | Good scenario-design framework; long scripts and prescriptive emotional interpretation may overreach. |

## Why these rewrites are different

- Reduced fixed word counts and avoided automatic full-panel simulations.
- Added evidence/assumption boundaries, explicit evaluation criteria, and testing thresholds.
- Added routing collision rules and a direct-answer bypass.
- Added platform-specific packaging and offline structural tests.

**Caveat:** better instructions do not guarantee better answers. See `evals/rubric.md` for model comparison and failure reporting.
